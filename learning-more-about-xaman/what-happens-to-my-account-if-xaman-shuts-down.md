# What happens to my account if Xaman shuts down?

Your account would be safe. Xaman is a self-custody wallet: your funds live on the XRP Ledger, not with Xaman, and the app keeps working even without us.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Xaman never holds your funds, and it doesn't depend on us.** Xaman will keep running whether or not XRPL Labs, the company behind Xaman, exists.
{% endhint %}

### Your funds live on the XRP Ledger

The XRP Ledger is run by computers (validators and full history nodes) all over the world, operated by many different people and organizations. Your funds exist as long as the XRP Ledger does. See [Xaman & natural disasters](https://help.xaman.app/app/getting-started-with-xaman/what-is-xumm/will-xumm-operate-after-a-natural-disaster) for how this works.

### Xaman works without us

Xaman runs on your phone, not on our servers. It connects to the XRP Ledger and signs your transactions right on your device, so even if XRPL Labs no longer existed, Xaman would keep working.

### Your account secret is your fallback

Your account secret (Secret Numbers, Family Seed, or Mnemonic) is the key to your account. If, for whatever reason, you can't use Xaman, your account secret lets you access your account from another wallet.

* **Write it down** and store it offline, somewhere safe.
* **Never share it** with anyone, including anyone claiming to be from Xaman. See [Self custody 101: Staying safe with Xaman](https://help.xaman.app/app/getting-started-with-xaman/self-custody-101-staying-safe-with-xaman).
* **Check that you have it.** You can test your account secret in Xaman to make sure it's correct. See [How to test your Account Secret](https://help.xaman.app/app/getting-started-with-xaman/how-to-test-your-account-secret). If you can't find your account secret, deal with it now, while you can still use Xaman. See [I've lost my account secret!](https://help.xaman.app/app/getting-started-with-xaman/ive-lost-my-account-secret)

### Moving your account to another wallet

If you ever want or need to use another wallet:

* **Secret Numbers** are a format created by Xaman. Not every wallet reads them, but you can convert them to a **Family Seed**, which most XRP Ledger wallets support. See [Moving your XRPL account to another wallet](https://help.xaman.app/app/learning-more-about-xaman/moving-your-xrpl-account-to-another-wallet).
* **Family Seeds** and **Mnemonics** can be entered directly into most XRP Ledger wallets.

Secret Numbers are an open, proposed standard, and the code to read and convert them is open source and publicly available, so it doesn't rely on Xaman.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Entering your account secret into any wallet, website, or device carries a risk of exposing it. Only use wallets you trust. See [Should I import my XRP Ledger account into Xaman?](https://help.xaman.app/app/getting-started-with-xaman/importing-your-account/should-i-import-my-xrp-ledger-account-into-xaman)
{% endhint %}

### What about Xaman cards?

Your cards don't depend on Xaman either. Xaman cards are made by Tangem, and they also work in the Tangem app, available on the App Store and Google Play. If you ever can't use Xaman, you can reach the account on your card there.

Card accounts work a little differently from the rest of this article:

* **There's no account secret to write down.** The secret never leaves the card, so your cards themselves are your fallback.
* **Keep your cards safe,** and keep your Primary and Backup cards in different places. See [Best security practices for the Xaman cards](https://help.xaman.app/app/xaman-tangem-cards/best-security-practices-using-a-xaman-card).
