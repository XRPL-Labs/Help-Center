# Setting up a Backup card

### Background

A Backup card is your safety net. It is a second Xaman card that can sign for your Primary account, so if one card is ever lost, stolen, or damaged, the other still gets you into your account. This article walks you through linking a Backup card, screen by screen, and explains what happens along the way.

The Backup card is not a copy of your Primary card. Each card keeps its own key inside its chip, and nothing is ever copied from one card to the other. Instead, Xaman uses an XRP Ledger feature called a regular key. A regular key is a second key that the XRP Ledger allows to sign for an account, alongside the account's own key. Your Backup card becomes that second key for your Primary account, while your Primary card keeps full control.

You can use this article when you set up new cards for the first time, when you add a Backup card later, or when you replace a lost one.&#x20;

### Before you get started

Check that you have these ready:

* Your Primary card's account, imported into Xaman and activated.
* A second Xaman card to use as your Backup card.
* Xaman version 5.4 or later, on a phone with NFC turned on.

Your Backup card's account does not need any XRP of its own. It never holds funds, and it only signs for your Primary account, so there is nothing to activate. If you are setting up brand-new cards, start with [_Setting up your Xaman cards_](setting-up-your-xaman-cards.md), which covers importing and activating your Primary card first.

### Set up your Backup card

When your Primary account has no Backup card, Xaman shows a reminder on it. The reminder is not an error, and it stays on your Primary account until a backup is set up for it. Tapping it starts the setup, and Xaman handles everything from there. The whole process takes only a minute or two.

To set up your Backup card:

1. Open your Primary account, and tap **No back-up card configured**.

Your Primary card approves the link, since only the Primary card can add a second key to its own account. Xaman takes care of the rest in the background, so there is no transaction for you to review. When you tap **Done**, Xaman returns you to your Primary account, and the **No back-up card configured** reminder is gone. That is your sign that both cards are set up and ready to use.

### Which card to carry day to day

A common setup is to keep the Primary card somewhere safe, such as a safe, a safe deposit box, or a locked drawer, and carry the Backup card for everyday signing. The Primary card is the master key to the account your money lives on, so it makes sense to keep it out of harm's way. If you lose the Backup card you carry, your Primary card can easily remove its link and still control everything.

Either way works, though. Both cards can sign for the same account, so choose the arrangement that fits your life. Whichever you choose, keep the two cards in different places, so a single fire, theft, or lost bag cannot take both.

### If you lose a card

Losing one card is exactly what a Backup card protects you from, since the other card still gets you into your account. The steps depend on which card is missing, and [Lost or damaged cards](../xaman-cards/lost-or-damaged-cards.md) walks you through each case. One thing is worth doing right now, while both cards are in your hands: write down your Primary account’s r-address and keep it somewhere safe. If you ever lose your phone and your Primary card together, you need it to reach your account with your Backup card. Xaman is a self-custody wallet, so we cannot recover a lost card for you.

### Frequently asked questions

**Is my Backup card a copy of my Primary card?**

No. Each card keeps its own key, and nothing is copied between them. Your Backup card is only allowed to sign for your Primary account, the way a second key opens the same door.

**Can I link more than one Backup card?**

No. The XRP Ledger allows one regular key per account, so each account can have one Backup card. Extra cards are still useful as spares, ready to become your new Backup card if you ever need one.

**Can I use a regular XRP Ledger account as my backup instead of a card?**

You can, though we recommend a second card. A regular account's account secret lives on a phone or on paper, not inside a chip, so it is easier to lose or steal than a card. If you still want to set one up, see [How to Rekey an XRP Ledger (XRPL) account](../learning-more-about-xaman/how-to-rekey-your-account.md).

**I do not see the No back-up card configured reminder. What should I do?**

First, make sure you have Xaman version 5.4 or later, since earlier versions used a separate xApp for this. If your Primary account already has a backup, the reminder does not appear. You can still contact us through the [Xaman Support xApp](https://xaman.app/detect/xapp:xumm.support-md) if something looks wrong.
