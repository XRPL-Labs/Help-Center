# Staking and earning yield with Xaman

### Background

Staking means locking up coins in return for rewards. There are two common kinds:

* On a proof-of-stake network, such as Ethereum or Cardano, validators lock up coins for a chance to add the next block. They earn a reward when they do.
* On some exchanges, you lend your coins to the exchange. It uses them to earn rewards and pays you part of what it earns.

The XRP Ledger (XRPL) is not a proof-of-stake network, so there is no staking on it. Its validators work together to confirm transactions, and they do not lock up XRP or earn rewards for it.

### Ways to earn with Xaman

Xaman does not run a staking or reward program. You can still earn from your account in a few ways, each run by a different network or company.

#### The Flare XRPFi Yield xApp

The **Flare XRPFi Yield** xApp moves your XRP to the Flare network, where it earns yield in a vault run by Upshift. You sign every step in Xaman, and you can withdraw your XRP back to your XRPL account. Fees apply when you deposit and withdraw.

This is not staking. Your XRP is converted to FXRP, a token on Flare that represents XRP one-to-one, and the vault earns yield with it. See _The Flare XRPFi Yield xApp_.

#### AMM pools on the XRP Ledger

The XRP Ledger has automated market maker (AMM) pools. When you add tokens to a pool, you earn a share of the trading fees the pool collects. The value of what you put in can also go down. See _Xaman and AMM_.

#### Balance Adjustment on Xahau

Xahau, a network that Xaman also supports, pays XAH holders a small yearly amount through its Balance Adjustment feature. You collect it with the **Balance Adjustment** xApp. See _Balance Adjustment_ for how it works.

### Staking offers from other projects

Some projects run their own staking schemes on the XRP Ledger. Most of them pay rewards in tokens they issue themselves. Xaman does not run, endorse, or support these schemes.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Before you join a staking scheme, find out how it pays its rewards. If a project cannot explain where the rewards come from, treat that as a warning sign. Only use funds you can afford to lose.
{% endhint %}

Crypto assets can change in value. This article explains how these features work and is not financial advice.
