# Importing your Xaman card

Importing a Xaman card takes about two minutes, and all you need is your phone and the card. There is no account secret to write down: the card creates its [account secret](#user-content-fn-1)[^1] on its own chip and keeps it there, and Xaman only ever talks to the card. Once the card is imported, its account appears in Xaman like any other, and you sign for it by holding the card against your phone.

Use these steps whenever you add a card: your pre-funded card from a new set, an extra card for a separate account, or one of your own cards on a new phone. If your cards have just arrived, [Setting up your Xaman cards](getting-started.md) takes you through the whole setup in order. Your Backup card does not need importing at all, since Xaman adds it for you when you link it.

### Before you get started

* A phone with NFC[^2], with NFC turned on. On an iPhone, NFC is always on. On Android, turn it on with the **NFC** tile in your quick settings, or in your phone's settings.
* The latest version of Xaman.
* Your Xaman card.

### Import your card

1. Tap **Settings** > **Accounts**.
2. Tap **+ Add account**.

<figure><img src="https://3221812686-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FMiHAzvIPISVuuzt0AeOR%2Fuploads%2Fy3brtv9IVgL7tIiQTPVQ%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=889577ac-32ad-4e5e-b9fd-83e178795321" alt="The Add account screen, with the Add Xaman Tangem card button" width="326"><figcaption><p>Add Xaman Tangem card starts the import</p></figcaption></figure>

3. Tap **Add Xaman Tangem card**.
4. Hold your card against the back of your phone until the scan finishes. A new card shows that it is brand new.

<figure><img src="https://3221812686-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FMiHAzvIPISVuuzt0AeOR%2Fuploads%2FM9rp8i88UeBFWUBtQ0I3%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=aecbddf1-9029-4b98-9881-47857d1f3ad5" alt="The screen confirming that the card is brand new, with a Continue button" width="293"><figcaption><p>A new card has never been used</p></figcaption></figure>

5. Tap **Continue**. The card creates its account secret on its own chip.
6. On the **Signature verification** screen, tap **Verify**.

<figure><img src="https://3221812686-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FMiHAzvIPISVuuzt0AeOR%2Fuploads%2F2qfKJrXG4N1YWSRoglCv%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=67207d1f-00b2-4c1c-8c6f-982d0ec30b3d" alt="The Signature verification screen, with a Verify button" width="333"><figcaption><p>Verify that the card signs correctly</p></figcaption></figure>

7. Hold your card against your phone again.
8. Check the r-address[^3] shown, and tap **Confirm**. This is the address your card controls.
9. If Xaman says the account is not activated yet, tap **Next, I understand**.
10. Enter a name for the account, such as _Main card_, and tap **Next**.
11. Tap **Hooray, let's go!**

Xaman takes you back to the **Home** screen, where your card's account is now selected. The name starts with a 💳, so you can tell card accounts apart at a glance.

### Activate the card's account

A new card creates a brand-new XRP Ledger account, and it needs to be activated[^4] before you can use it. Activating means sending it enough XRP to cover its reserve, which the account keeps for as long as it exists.

* **Your pre-funded card: already covered.** It comes with enough XRP to activate its account. Tap **Activate your account** to claim it.
* **Any other card you plan to use as its own account: send it XRP.** Send enough XRP to cover the reserve from another account or an exchange. [Activating an account (XRPL)](../getting-xrp-into-xaman/how-to-activate-a-new-xrpl-account/) explains how.
* **Your Backup card: never.** It only signs for your Primary account, so its own account never needs activating.

### If Xaman says the card was used before

Xaman checks every card the first time you tap it, to confirm the card is genuine and whether it has been set up before. If it has, Xaman shows a warning instead of the brand-new message.

<figure><img src="https://3221812686-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FMiHAzvIPISVuuzt0AeOR%2Fuploads%2FICBBQEaL4PsfFgYEbCwD%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=1683e5bd-8729-4bf1-81bf-9ed1d9a7c84a" alt="The warning that the card was used before, with Cancel, Contact support, and Continue anyway buttons" width="289"><figcaption><p>The warning for a card set up before</p></figcaption></figure>

What to do depends on whose card it is:

* **One of your own cards: tap Continue anyway.** The warning is expected when you import a card you set up before, such as on a new phone or after removing it from Xaman. The import then carries on from step 5.
* **A card you expected to be new: tap Cancel.** Do not import it, and contact us through the Xaman Support xApp before you use it. [My card package arrived opened](my-card-package-arrived-opened.md) explains what to check.

**Contact support** on this screen opens your email app with a message to support@xaman.app. Our support team will never ask for your account secret, or ask you to tap your card to fix your account. Anyone who asks is trying to take your funds.

### If the import does not work

Most import problems come down to NFC, and each one shows its own message:

* **"Your phone does not seem to support NFC": use a different phone.** Your phone has no NFC reader, so it cannot talk to the card.
* **The Add Xaman Tangem card button is missing: turn on NFC.** Xaman only shows the button when NFC is on. Turn it on, then close and reopen Xaman.
* **"Unable to verify the signature!": try the scan again.** Hold the card flat against the back of your phone, and keep it still until the scan finishes. If it keeps failing, the card may be faulty, so contact us.
* **"Unexpected error occurred.": restart Xaman and try again.** If it keeps happening, send us a session log, as described in [How to post a Session Log](../using-the-xaman-app/how-to-post-a-session-log.md).

### Frequently asked questions

**Do I need my account secret to import a card?**

No. The card creates its account secret on its own chip, and the secret never leaves it. You never see it, type it, or write it down, so there is nothing to lose or share.

**Can I import the same card on more than one phone?**

Yes. The card holds the key, so you can import it on any phone with NFC and Xaman. Each phone after the first shows the used-before warning, which is expected for your own card.

**Do I need to import my Backup card?**

No. When you link your Backup card, Xaman scans it and adds its account for you. See [Setting up a Backup card](setting-up-a-backup-card.md).

[^1]: The secret that controls your account, as Secret Numbers, a Family Seed, or a Mnemonic. Never share it with anyone.

[^2]: The technology your phone uses for tap-to-pay. Xaman uses it to talk to your Xaman cards.

[^3]: Your account's address on the XRP Ledger. It always starts with "r", and it is safe to share.

[^4]: Sending an account its first XRP, enough to cover its reserve. Until then, the account does not exist on the XRP Ledger.
