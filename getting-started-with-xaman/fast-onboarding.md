# Fast onboarding

When you create your first account with a fresh install of Xaman, the app shows an **Onboarding** screen with two options.

* **Write your account secret down now (safer):** You write all 8 rows of your account secret on paper, then you re-enter them in the app. After that, you choose the security level for the account.
* **Fast onboarding (risky):** You skip the write-down step for now. The app keeps the numbers and shows a red **Back up** button on the account home screen.

The app explains the risky option in its own words: "Write down & re-enter later: all your funds are at risk until you do so!"

### What changes with fast onboarding

* The app skips the steps where you write down and re-enter the account secret.
* The security level is fixed to **Standard** (sign with FaceID / Passcode). You cannot choose **Extra security** (sign with a password) for a fast-onboarded account.
* The app stores the 8 rows of your account secret in the account record on your device. That is why the app can show them to you again.
* The app shows a red warning at the top of the account home screen.
* Until you have the numbers written down, you cannot recover the account if your phone is lost, stolen, or damaged.

If you choose fast onboarding, the app asks you to confirm: "Are you sure you want to proceed? Until you write down your account secret, you will NOT be able to recover your account if your phone gets lost, stolen or damaged." You continue by tapping **I accept the risk**.

### The red Back up button

The home screen of a fast-onboarded account shows this red banner:

> "You are unprotected in case of lost, stolen or damaged phone!"

next to a red **Back up** button.

The banner appears only on accounts created with fast onboarding. The app does not show it for imported accounts.

### How to use Back up

1. Open Xaman and select the account.
2. Tap the red **Back up** button.
3. The app shows your secret numbers, one row at a time, labeled A to H. The screen says "Secret numbers of row A" and "Please write down all the numbers". The numbers are read-only on this screen.
4. Write all 8 rows on paper. Store the paper in a safe place. Do not take a photo of the numbers. Do not save them in a note, email, or cloud folder.
5. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move between the rows. Tap **Done** after row H.
6. The app asks you to type each row again, from your paper. The screen says "Confirm the numbers of row A" and "Please repeat the numbers". If a row does not match, the app says "The numbers entered do not match the requested row of numbers." Correct the row and continue.
7. After row H matches, the app says: "Wonderful! You're all set! Whenever you have to recover your account, you can do so by importing the account secret you just wrote down."

### What happens after Back up

* The red banner goes away.
* The app has no other screen that shows the numbers. From that moment, your paper is the only copy of your account secret.
* The security level stays Standard. The app offers no option to add a signing password to a fast-onboarded account later.

### If you lose the numbers

Your account secret is not on the ledger and Xaman support has no tool to read it back.

Read: [I've lost my account secret](https://help.xaman.app/app/learning-more-about-xaman/ive-lost-my-account-secret.md)

**Scam warning.** Xaman staff will never ask for your account secret. Anyone who claims to be Xaman and asks for your 8 rows is a scammer. Do not type the numbers into any website. Anyone who has the 8 rows can move your funds.

### FAQ

**Why don't I see the fast onboarding option?** The option appears only when the app has no account on the device. It appears for your first account only. Adding a second account or importing an account skips the choice.

**I chose fast onboarding. Can I add a signing password now?** No. The app shows the **Change Password** option only to accounts that were created with a signing password. There is no in-app way to switch a Standard account to Extra security.

**Can Xaman support show me my numbers?** No. The numbers exist only in the app record on your device. They are not part of your on-ledger account.

**Does Back up work without internet?** Yes. The app reads the numbers from the record on the device. It needs no network connection.
