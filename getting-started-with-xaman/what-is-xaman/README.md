# 💸 What is Xaman?

Xaman (formerly Xumm) is a self-custody wallet app for the XRP Ledger (XRPL) and Xahau. XRPL Labs develops it. You install it on your phone, and use it to manage your account: view your balances, send and receive XRP and tokens, and sign transactions.

### What self-custody means

In Xaman, you control your account. Your account secret is stored on your phone, encrypted, and protected by your passcode. Xaman cannot see it, use it, or recover it for you.

Your XRP and tokens are not stored in the app. They live in your account on the XRP Ledger. Xaman is how you view your account and sign transactions for it.

This gives you full control, and full responsibility. If you lose your account secret and your phone, no one can restore your account. Keep a backup of your account secret somewhere safe and offline. See [_Understanding self-custody_](understanding-self-custody.md).

### What you can do with Xaman

With Xaman, you can:

* See the XRP, tokens, and NFTs in your account, with their balances, on the home screen.
* Send and receive XRP and tokens, using **Send** and **Receive**.
* Swap one asset for another, using **Swap**.
* Add tokens to your account with **Add a token**, which creates a Trust Line.
* Buy and sell XRP through third-party providers.
* Use xApps, small apps that run inside Xaman, from **xApps** in the bottom navigation bar.
* Sign transactions for websites and apps that work with the XRP Ledger.
* Manage several accounts in one app, and switch between them on the home screen.
* Use Xaman cards to keep an account secret on a card instead of your phone.

### How signing works

Every transaction you make in Xaman needs your signature. Before you sign, Xaman shows the **Review transaction** screen with the details of the transaction.

Check the destination, the **Amount**, and the **Network & Service Fee** on this screen. When everything is correct, slide to accept. Once the XRP Ledger confirms a transaction, it cannot be reversed, by you or by the Xaman team.

### Networks

Xaman works with two networks: the XRP Ledger and Xahau. Switch between them with the **XRPL** and **XAHAU** buttons at the top of the home screen. The same account can exist on both networks, but its balances on each network are separate.

### Fees

Every transaction on the XRP Ledger and Xahau has a small network fee. It goes to the network, not to Xaman. Some transactions also have a Xaman service fee, which the signing screen shows before you sign. See [_Understanding Xaman Service Fees_](understanding-the-xaman-service-fee.md).

### Frequently asked questions

**Is Xaman the same as Xumm?**

Yes. Xumm was renamed Xaman. The app, your accounts, and your account secrets are the same.

**Does Xaman hold my XRP?**

No. Your XRP is in your account on the XRP Ledger. Xaman stores your account secret on your phone, so you can sign transactions. See [_Where are your funds stored?_](where-are-your-funds-stored.md)

**Can Xaman recover my account if I lose my account secret?**

No. Xaman never has a copy of your account secret, so it cannot restore your account. See [_I've lost my account secret!_](../../security/ive-lost-my-account-secret.md)

**Is Xaman owned by Ripple?**

No. Xaman is developed by XRPL Labs, which is independent of Ripple. See [_Xaman vs. Ripple_](../../about-xaman/is-xaman-owned-by-ripple.md).
