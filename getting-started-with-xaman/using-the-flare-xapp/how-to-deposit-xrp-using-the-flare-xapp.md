# How to Deposit XRP using the Flare xApp

This flow converts your XRP into **FXRP** on the Flare network, where it earns yield. You will sign **two** transactions during the process.&#x20;

**Step 1 — Open the Flare xApp**&#x20;

Launch Xaman, tap the **xApp** button at the bottom of the screen, then tap **Flare XRPFi Yield**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1) (1) (1) (1) (1) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2 — Enter your deposit amount**&#x20;

Decide how much XRP you would like to deposit, enter the amount, and press **Request deposit**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1) (1) (1) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3 — Sign twice** You will be asked to sign **two** transactions in sequence:<br>

1. **First signature** — sends your XRP to Flare and mints FXRP on the Flare network.
2. **Press Deposit Now**, then **second signature** — deposits your FXRP into the Flare vault where it begins earning yield.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (98).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 4 — Done**&#x20;

You will see a short loading screen while the deposit completes. Your FXRP is now in the vault earning yield. You can view your balance anytime in the Flare xApp.

#### What happens next?

* Your FXRP accrues yield on the Flare network.
* To withdraw, open the Flare xApp and use the **Redeem** flow (sends FXRP back to XRP in your Xaman wallet).
* Withdrawals are subject to network conditions and may take a few minutes.



#### Frequently Asked Questions

**"Why can't I deposit XRP into the fund?"**&#x20;

Common causes:

* You entered an amount below the minimum (check the field for the minimum).
* Your XRP balance is insufficient after network fees.
* The Flare vault is temporarily full (rare).

If none of those apply, create a support ticket with a screenshot of the error and your r-address.\
