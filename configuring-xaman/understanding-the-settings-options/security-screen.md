# Security screen

Xaman protects your accounts with built-in security features. This page explains the options you can set on the Security screen.

### How to find the Security screen

Open Xaman. Press the **Settings** button at the bottom right corner of the screen. See [Finding the Settings screen](https://help.xaman.app/app/configuring-xaman/understanding-the-settings-options) for the full walkthrough.

Next, select **Security**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Security Screen shot.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Change passcode

This option changes the 6-digit passcode you use to open Xaman.

You must know the current passcode to change it. If you forgot the passcode, see [How to reset the 6-digit passcode](https://help.xaman.app/app/learning-more-about-xaman/how-to-reset-the-6-digit-passcode).

### Auto lock

This option sets how long Xaman stays open before it asks for your passcode or biometric unlock again.

The default setting is 1 minute. You can set a time from 0 seconds to 1 week.

For example:

* **0 seconds:** Every time you close Xaman and open it again, you must enter your passcode or use biometric unlock.
* **1 week:** You can open Xaman for 1 week after you last unlocked it.

### Biometric authentication

This option turns on or off biometric unlock (Face ID or fingerprint).

If you turn this option off, you can open Xaman only with your 6-digit passcode.

### Erase data

If this option is on, Xaman erases all account data after 10 failed passcode attempts. Xaman then resets to a fresh install and removes all accounts.

To get your accounts back, you import them again with your Secret Numbers. See [How to import your account](https://help.xaman.app/app/getting-started-with-xaman/importing-your-account).

This option is off by default.

### Hide balance by default

This option hides your r-address, your XRP balance and your token balances.

This option is useful when you are in a crowded place. It stops people near you from seeing your account balance.

### Block taking screenshots (Android only)

This option stops screenshots from Xaman.

If you need a screenshot, turn this option off and take your screenshot. When you close Xaman and open it again, the option turns back on by itself. This option is on by default.

### **Background information...**

While Xumm handles most of the heavy security behind the scenes, there are a few options left to users to decide for themselves.

Here is how the Security settings work and how to configure them.

**How to get to the Security screen**

Launch Xumm, press the **Settings** button in the lower right corner of the screen.

![mceclip2.png](https://drtc9zr.dlvr.cloud/hc/article_attachments/5133588104850/mceclip2.png)

Next, select the **Security** option.

![mceclip1.png](https://drtc9zr.dlvr.cloud/hc/article_attachments/5133562651666/mceclip1.png)

**Security options**

**Change passcode**

This option allows users to change the 6-digit passcode needed to access the Xumm application.

In order to change the passcode, you **must know** the existing 6 digit passcode.

If you have lost/forgotten the passcode, please refer to this article:

* [How to reset the 6 digit passcode in Xumm](https://support.xumm.app/hc/en-us/articles/5136606389394)

**Auto Lock**

This option controls the amount of time Xumm waits before requiring the 6 digit passcode/Biometric authentication be entered to unlock the app.

The default setting for Auto lock is 1 minute but the range of settings is from 0 seconds to 1 week.

For example, if you set Auto lock to 0 seconds, every time you exit Xumm, you will need to enter your 6-digit passcode/Biometric authentication before you can get into Xumm again. If you set Auto lock to 1 week, you can exit Xumm and gain access to Xumm for 1 week before you need to enter your 6 digit passcode/Biometric authentication again.

**Biometric authentication**

This feature allows you to enable and disable Biometric authentication. If disabled, Xumm will only accept the 6-digit passcode to unlock the application.

**Erase data**

When you enable this option, you are limited to 10 failed attempts to enter your 6 digit passcode. After the 10th failed attempt, Xumm will remove all account information and reset Xumm to a factory fresh installation. All accounts will be removed from Xumm. At this point you will need to re-import each XRPL account back into Xumm using the secret numbers. By default, this option is disabled.

**Hide balance by default**

By enabling this option, your r-address, XRP balance and your token balances are hidden. This option can be useful if you are in crowded place and do not want others looking over your shoulder and seeing your account balance.

![mceclip0.png](https://drtc9zr.dlvr.cloud/hc/article_attachments/5136462320146/mceclip0.png)

**Block taking screenshots (Android only)**

This options prevents screenshots from being taken. Should you ever need to take a screenshot in Xumm, just disable this option and you should be able to take screenshots. When you exit Xumm and re-launch, Block taking screenshots is automatically enabled again. This option is enabled by default.
