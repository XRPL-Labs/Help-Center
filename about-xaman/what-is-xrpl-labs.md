# About XRPL Labs

### Background

XRPL Labs is the company that builds and maintains Xaman (formerly Xumm), the self-custody[^1] wallet for the XRP Ledger (XRPL) and Xahau. This page explains who XRPL Labs is, what that means for your account, and who is on the team. It applies to Xaman on both networks, since it is about the company rather than an account.

### Who XRPL Labs is

Wietse Wind discovered the XRP Ledger in 2016 and spent the next few years building tools for its community. In 2019, he founded XRPL Labs to turn that work into a company. Besides Xaman itself, the team builds the [Xaman xApps](https://help.xaman.app/app/xapps/xaman-xapps) that run inside the app, such as Account Merge and DEX Trade. XRPL Labs also led the development of Hooks, the feature that lets Xahau run small programs on accounts, and was one of the organizations that launched Xahau in 2023.

People often ask whether XRPL Labs is part of Ripple. _Xaman vs. Ripple_ answers that question in full, so this page leaves it there.

### What this means for your account

Building the app does not give XRPL Labs control of your account. Xaman is a self-custody wallet, so your [account secret](#user-content-fn-2)[^2] stays on your phone or your Xaman card, and only you can sign for your account. Your funds are on the XRP Ledger or Xahau, not inside the app or with the company.

That protection works both ways. XRPL Labs cannot move, freeze, or recover your funds, and it cannot reverse a transaction once the network has confirmed it. It also means your account does not depend on the company: _What happens to my account if Xaman shuts down?_ explains how you keep access no matter what.
